GHAR NA NIYAM · HOUSE RULES

Terms

Draft for review. Have these terms checked by a lawyer before launch.

People of Gandhinagar is a small 3D city for meeting people from Gandhinagar. It is an early demo. By walking in you agree to these rules. If you do not agree, please do not use it.

The short version

  • Be yourself, be kind, and treat people the way you would at a chai stall.
  • You decide what goes on your profile. Assume strangers can read it.
  • It is an early demo. Things will break, change and sometimes get reset.

Who can use it

Anyone can look around. To sign in and become visible you must be 18 or older and use an email address or WhatsApp number that is your own. One account per person.

Your profile and what you post

  • Use your real name or the name people actually know you by. Do not pretend to be another person or a company you do not represent.
  • Your profile, plans and messages belong to you. You give us permission to store them and show them inside the app, as described on the Privacy page, so the app can work.
  • You are responsible for what you write and for the links you add.

How to behave

Please do not harass, threaten, stalk or demean anyone, or keep contacting someone who has not replied; post anything hateful, sexual, violent or illegal; spam people with waves, requests, plans or sales pitches; collect other people's details, scrape the app, or use what you find here to contact people elsewhere against their wishes; or run automated accounts or try to break or overload the app.

Waves, connections, chat and plans

  • A wave or a connect request is an invitation, not an obligation. Nobody owes you a reply.
  • There are daily limits on waves and requests, and unanswered requests expire.
  • You can block and report anyone. A report sends the owner a copy of your chat with that person so it can be reviewed.
  • Plans are made between people. We do not organise, check or supervise them. If you meet someone in person, meet in a public place, tell a friend, and use your own judgment.

AI residents, shops and listings

Some characters are AI residents that keep the streets from feeling empty. They are labelled AI, and their replies are written by AI. Shop prices, events and listings in the city are samples unless they say otherwise. The ₹ you earn in missions and gigs is play money; it is not real money and cannot be paid out.

AI twins

A twin's replies are written by AI from what the member shared. They are not statements, promises, prices, offers or agreements by that member. You are responsible for what you teach your twin: nothing false or harmful about other people, nothing sexual, no contact details. We may switch off or remove a twin that breaks these rules.

Changes

We may change these rules as the app grows. We will update the date at the top when we do.

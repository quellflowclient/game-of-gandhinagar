Who we are

People of Gandhinagar is an interactive city experience operated under the name gameofgandhinagar. This notice explains how information is handled when you visit the website, explore the game or use its account, social and optional features.

Questions about this notice or your information can be sent to support@gamail.com.

Information you provide

Account and profile
Your email is used for sign-in. If you create a profile, you may add a name, introduction, interests, work details, links, character choices, area and availability.
Conversations and interactions
We process the messages, waves, connection requests, feedback and reports you choose to submit. A report may include relevant conversation context.
AI features
When enabled, AI conversations use your prompt, conversation context and relevant character or public-profile information. An AI twin also uses the notes its owner provides.
Virtual campus passes
A virtual pass can include the name, enrolment or contact details, address and visit information you enter. The game saves these details in your browser for reuse. The pass is a game feature, not official college identity verification.

Browser and game information

You can explore without an account. Visitors are not shown as named player profiles. The website still needs to download files and connect to services; hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details, timestamps and error logs.

While a signed-in player is visible, the service uses their position inside the game, chosen vehicle, online status and current game area to place the character and support nearby interactions. These virtual coordinates are separate from device GPS. Closing a session or allowing an online marker to expire does not automatically delete related operational records.

Browser storage keeps preferences, sign-in state, local progress and virtual-pass information. In a clearly labelled demo mode, demonstration records are stored in that browser. Clearing site data removes local records and can sign you out; it does not erase records held by the online service.

Optional device location

The game asks separately before accessing your precise device location. If you allow it, the browser can provide latitude, longitude and location accuracy while the game runs. When signed in, this information may be sent to gameofgandhinagar for the location-sharing feature and related service support.

Location sharing is optional. You can keep exploring without it. Precise device location is not displayed to other players as a public profile field or player marker.

You can refuse the request or revoke location permission in your browser settings to stop new location access. Changing permission does not automatically delete information already submitted. Contact us if you want to ask about that information.

How information is used

  • Sign you in, maintain your account and remember your preferences.
  • Render the city, show visible players and provide the interactions you choose.
  • Deliver messages, voice features and AI replies.
  • Respond to support requests, investigate reports and protect the service against misuse.
  • Maintain reliability and meet applicable legal obligations.

Weather requests use Gandhinagar’s fixed city coordinates. They do not require you to allow precise device location.

Sharing and service providers

Information you place on a visible profile can be shown to other members. City-chat messages are public within the app, including to people browsing without an account. Private chats are intended for their participants; relevant content may also be processed to deliver the service, respond to a report or meet a legal requirement.

We use infrastructure and feature providers as needed: Vercel for hosting, Supabase for sign-in and online records, and Anthropic for enabled AI replies. AI prompts, relevant context and twin notes may be sent to Anthropic to generate a reply. If an AI service is unavailable, the game may use local fallback replies.

Browser requests may also reach Google Fonts, weather providers and voice-connection services. Voice connections can use STUN/TURN services, including Google or Cloudflare, to establish or relay a connection. Providers and authorised service personnel may process relevant information for operation, support, security and legal purposes. Processing may occur outside your country.

Information may be disclosed where necessary to comply with law, address a legal claim or protect people and the service.

Microphone and voice

Voice calls and table conversations request microphone access when you start, join or accept a voice feature. The game stops its microphone tracks when you end the call or leave the voice session. The app does not provide call recording.

Other participants receive the audio you choose to share. Connection services process signalling and network information needed to connect participants. Optional browser speech input, where used, is also subject to your browser or speech provider’s processing. You can deny microphone permission and continue using non-voice features.

Your controls and requests

  • Edit your profile and visibility through Menu → Profile.
  • Choose what you put in profiles, public chat and AI prompts. Avoid unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Use block and report controls for unwanted interactions.
  • End a voice session, revoke browser permissions or sign out when you wish.
  • Clear browser site data to remove local preferences and virtual passes.

You can contact support@gamail.com to ask about access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal or another privacy concern. Rights and any exceptions depend on the law that applies. We may need to verify your identity before handling a request. Never send your password or one-time sign-in code.

Retention and security

Signing out, hiding a profile or closing the game does not automatically delete account, message, report or other service records. Some items stop appearing after a time window; that is not by itself a promise that the underlying record has been deleted. Contact us about retention or deletion of information linked to you.

Records may need to be retained for service operation, security, dispute handling, backups or legal obligations. We use access controls and service-provider protections, but no internet service or storage system can promise absolute security.

Age and policy changes

Account and interactive social features are intended for people aged 18 or older. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through the service, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

We may update this notice as the service changes. The date at the top identifies the latest version. If a change requires a new permission or consent, that will be requested separately where required.

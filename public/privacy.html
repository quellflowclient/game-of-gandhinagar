People of Gandhinagar is a small 3D city you can walk around to meet people from Gandhinagar. This page says what the app keeps about you, who can see it, and how you can take it back.

Who runs this

The People of Gandhinagar team, Gandhinagar, India. Contact: [add your contact email before launch].

If you only look around

  • Nobody can see you. You are counted only as "just looking", with no name and no position.
  • Nothing about you is written to our database.
  • Your passport (stamps, streak, missions), play money and sound settings are saved in your own browser only. Clearing your browser data removes them.

If you sign in and become visible

Signing in uses a one-time email code or sign-in link. Data is kept with a hosted database and sign-in service (Supabase).

AI

AI resident and AI twin replies are written by Claude, a model from Anthropic. What you type to an AI resident or a twin, and the twin's notes, are sent to Anthropic to write the reply.

Analytics

If analytics is switched on, we count page views and feature use to improve the app. No chats are sent to analytics.

Your choices

  • Switch "Show me to other people" off at any time.
  • Edit or clear your profile and your twin at any time.
  • Ask us to delete your account and everything linked to it.
